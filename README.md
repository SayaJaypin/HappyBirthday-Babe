# 🦫 💕 A Birthday Story — Custom Capybara Web Experience

A high-end, responsive, interactive romantic birthday website created for **September 19th, 2004**. Designed with an Apple & Pinterest aesthetic featuring handcrafted glassmorphism visuals, smooth CSS/JS micro-animations, audio sync, and a code-built 3D Capybara mascot.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **100% Code-Generated 3D Capybara Mascot**: Built with pure HTML, CSS layers, linear/radial gradients, and transforms. Includes reactive states: idle breathing, eye blinking, ear wiggles, dancing to music, holding hearts/flowers, waving, and reacting to clicks.
2. **Interactive Audio Player**: Visualizer with animated equalizer bars, spinning vinyl disk, progress bar, play/pause, volume control, and auto-sync with Capybara dance animation. Pre-configured for *Always — Daniel Caesar*.
3. **Aspect-Ratio-Aware Masonry Gallery**: Automatic grid adaptation supporting:
   - **5 Photos** in `3:4` aspect ratio (`1.jpg` – `5.jpg`)
   - **2 Photos** in `9:16` aspect ratio (`6.jpg` – `7.jpg`)
   - **3 Photos** in `4:3` aspect ratio (`8.jpg` – `10.jpg`)
   - Complete with interactive Lightbox, full zoom, and smooth hover micro-interactions.
4. **Interactive 3D HTML/CSS Birthday Cake**: Multi-layered decorated cake with flickering flame candles. Supports candle-blowing action (tap/click or microphone trigger), smoke particles, and confetti celebrations.
5. **15+ Interactive Gift Box & Love Jar Modules**: Virtual coupons, secret love envelopes with wax seals, opening chocolate box, wish bottle, love jar, promise cards, and plushie interactions.
6. **Dynamic Particle Background**: Canvas-driven ambient particles featuring floating hearts, soft glowing stars, butterflies, and sunset fireflies.
7. **Romantic Interactive Letter**: Unfoldable letter with wax seal animation, paper texturing, and realistic typing effects.
8. **Responsive & Mobile-First**: Optimized for Android, iOS, tablets, and desktop displays.
